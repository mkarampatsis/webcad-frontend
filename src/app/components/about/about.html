Our Story

Founded by a consortium of structural engineers and software architects, Metro-Bridge-2024 was born out of a need for more rigorous, mathematically certain design tools. We recognized that the bridge between conceptual drafting and real-world structural stability was often fraught with data silos.

Today, our suite of tools is used by global engineering firms to design some of the most challenging infrastructure projects in the modern world, from urban sky-bridges to deep-water foundations.
